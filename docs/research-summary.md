# Research summary

Research conducted to inform the positioning, content and factual boundaries of the
Midpoint Tech website. Sources: the live Midpoint Midrand website
(https://www.mid-point.co.za/, reviewed August 2026) and the project brief supplied
for Midpoint Tech at 300 Janadel Avenue.

## Verified facts

- **Midpoint Tech's address** is 300 Janadel Avenue, Halfway House, Midrand, Gauteng,
  1685, South Africa (per project brief).
- The **existing Midpoint estate** ("Midpoint Midrand") is a separate, established
  property at **162 Tonetti Street, Halfway House, Midrand, 1685** — not 300 Janadel
  Avenue. The two addresses must never be conflated.
- The existing Midpoint estate offers **offices, OnPoint serviced offices and
  warehouse/industrial space**, plus amenities (cafés, gym, padel courts, walking/
  running/cycling trails, a restaurant/bar, corporate accommodation) — some of which
  the parent site states are still under development.
- The existing Midpoint estate is **owned/developed by Blend Property Group**, a South
  African commercial property company established in 2006 (per Midpoint's own FAQ and
  footer).
- The existing Midpoint estate sits on the **N1 corridor between Johannesburg and
  Pretoria**, with stated distances of approximately 31 km to Pretoria, 25 km to
  Johannesburg and 21 km to OR Tambo International Airport, and ~1,470 m of N1
  frontage. These figures apply to 162 Tonetti Street specifically and **must not be
  presented as verified for 300 Janadel Avenue** without separate confirmation.
- Midpoint's existing contact channels are +27 11 380 9400 and
  boitumelo@blendproperty.co.za — these are the parent estate's leasing contacts, not
  confirmed as Midpoint Tech's dedicated line.
- Midpoint's own site explicitly states some imagery is conceptual/illustrative and
  that buildings are under construction — a pattern this project follows for placeholder
  media.

## Reasonable positioning recommendations

- Position Midpoint Tech as a **distinct, technology-focused sub-brand** of the wider
  Midpoint portfolio — "part of the Midpoint story," not a rebrand of the existing
  estate.
- Lead with **Midrand's position between Johannesburg and Pretoria** as a locational
  strength, without quoting the Tonetti Street-specific drive-time figures for the
  Janadel Avenue address.
- Emphasise **flexibility, scalability and a technology-oriented tenant mix** as the
  primary differentiator versus a generic office-to-let listing.
- Keep the **community/ecosystem proposition aspirational but honest** — describe
  intent rather than asserting an accelerator, funding access or a scheduled events
  programme that does not yet exist.
- Reuse Midpoint's **parent-brand credibility** (established 2006, Blend Property
  Group) as reassurance for larger, risk-averse tenants, without describing Midpoint
  Tech's specific building grade, certifications or infrastructure until confirmed.
